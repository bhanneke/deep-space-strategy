Parameters

Every number our models can use, one row each: the price of platinum, the cost per kilogram to orbit, the record of lunar landings, the power a lunar reactor should deliver. Each row names its source, says what kind of number it is (a measurement, a list price, an estimate or a company's goal) and whether someone has read it at the source.

ParameterValueYearStatus
What the labels mean
measured
An observed figure: market data, a flight result, a count.
estimated
A statistical or econometric estimate, such as a demand elasticity.
list price
A price the seller publishes.
contract
The value of a signed contract.
target
A goal a company or agency has announced; nothing has achieved it yet.
model
The output of an engineering or economic model.
derived
Our arithmetic on other rows.
verified
Someone opened the source and read the number there.
to check
The number comes from a search result, a secondary source or our arithmetic; nobody has read it at the primary source yet.
sources disagree
Two sources give different numbers; the note says which.

Download the register as parameters.json. The register grows each week; a row never changes its value, and a newer figure enters as a new row.
