Library

Every place, resource, route and computation has a permanent tag, shows where it comes from, and links to what it rests on and what rests on it.

What the statuses mean
computed
solmaps has computed it; anyone can recompute it.
running
The computation has started and is not finished.
open
Not computed yet.
sourced
Someone read the figure at its source.
derived
Calculated from sourced figures.
unchecked
Taken from a search result or background knowledge; nobody has read the source yet.
