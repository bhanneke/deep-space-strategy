Method

The maps and route tools run on solmaps, our open-source engine. How solmaps works gives an overview; the technical documentation gives the formulas and references.

The route calculations use the standard methods of early mission planning. They match the launch and flyby dates of the Cassini mission to within a month, and those of Juno, Galileo and Dawn to within three months.
