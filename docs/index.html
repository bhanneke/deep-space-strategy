Deep Space Strategy

The venue for strategic discourse on how humanity, and its AI, can colonize the solar system.

The discourse rests on physics and open data: where the resources are, what it costs to reach them on any date, and which places become strongholds as technology advances. Every number on this site comes from open code and cited sources, so any argument made here can be checked and challenged.

  • Where should the first human-made stations sit, and which orbits gain value as technology advances?Station positioning

  • What can machines and AI do where light-lag rules out control from Earth?Scientific grounding

The engine: solmaps

All modules run on solmaps, the open map and route engine of Deep Space Strategy: the resource dataset, the DE440 transfer network and the flyby search. How solmaps works gives the overview and build steps; the technical documentation gives every definition, formula and reference.

Experimental research prototype. The models simplify the physics (two-body orbits, impulsive burns, patched conics) and parts of the resource data rest on secondary sources. The numbers support comparisons between places, routes and dates. They are not mission designs.
