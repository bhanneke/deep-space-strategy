Research

The literature behind Deep Space Strategy: papers, reports, books and filings on space economics, resources, transport, law and strategy. Each work lists its topics, our short summary where someone has read it, the abstract from OpenAlex and a BibTeX entry. A second list shows the works a search of OpenAlex found since 2006 that nobody has reviewed yet.

Data

Every list on this page comes from plain JSON files that anyone can download or query from code, without a key: research.json (the library), candidates.json (the review list), topics.json and stats.json. The parameter register sits in parameters.json. AI agents find a short guide in llms.txt.

Abstracts, citation counts and the review list come from OpenAlex (CC0); OpenAlex stores abstracts as an inverted index, and this site rebuilds the text from it. Summaries are our own. The library grows each week.
