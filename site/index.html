Deep Space Strategy

Strategy for a settled solar system: where the resources are, what it costs to reach them on any date until 2100, and which places become strongholds as a result. Deep Space Strategy starts in Earth orbit and reaches the outer planets.

Experimental research prototype. The models simplify the physics (two-body orbits, impulsive burns, patched conics) and parts of the resource data rest on secondary sources. The numbers support comparisons between places, routes and dates. They are not mission designs.

The engine: solmaps

All modules run on solmaps, the open map and route engine of Deep Space Strategy: the resource dataset, the DE440 transfer network and the flyby search. How solmaps works gives the overview and build steps; the technical documentation gives every definition, formula and reference.

Deep Space Strategy is a research programme by Björn Hanneke. Code (MIT) and data (CC BY 4.0) at github.com/bhanneke/solmaps.
