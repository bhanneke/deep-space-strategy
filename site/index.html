solmaps

A timetable of routes through the solar system: where the resources are, what it costs to reach them on any date until 2100, and which places become strongholds as a result.

Experimental research prototype. The models are simplified (two-body orbits, impulsive burns, patched conics) and parts of the resource data rest on secondary sources. The numbers support comparisons between places, routes and dates. They are not mission designs. How solmaps is built explains the data, the models and their limits.

3D atlasThe solar system in 3D with resources, transfer arcs from or to any site, and ships you can launch. Resource atlasEvery planet, moon and asteroid population with its resources, evidence level and source. Growth mapRoute costs by departure date, flyby shortcuts, propellant hubs and depot orbits. How it is builtData sources, ephemerides, transfer model, flyby search, validation and limits. Technical documentationThe temporal transfer graph: definitions, every formula, algorithms, validation and references.
